# Game art: character and elevator polish

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool; no CLI/API fallback. Reference assets belong to this game. The generated PNGs have native transparency and are rendered without runtime body warping. Existing reference atlases are preserved.

## Final saved assets

- `static/play/eat-the-boss/assets/career-redrawn.png`: six sprites, 1536 × 1024. Promotion bodies and final CEO poses.
- `static/play/eat-the-boss/assets/elevator.png`: closed/open elevator atlas, 1536 × 1024. The renderer slides cropped full-width door leaves behind the jambs.

## Character prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite atlas for a 2D browser game. Use reference 1 for the young adult male employee identity and polished pixel-art style, reference 2 for final poses. Create exactly SIX isolated full-body sprites arranged in a rigid THREE columns by TWO rows grid, landscape 1536x1024. Each cell 512x512, generous empty gap, no overlap, no labels, no text. Truly transparent background, no checkerboard drawn into image, no ground shadows. Maintain consistent small blue-eyed face, spiky brown hair, thick dark navy pixel outlines and detailed shaded pixel-art. Keep all heads similar pixel size, all standing bodies about 460px tall with feet aligned at y=490 in their own cell. Redesign body anatomy, do NOT horizontally stretch the original. TOP LEFT: slightly chubby employee after first promotion, white rolled-sleeve shirt, teal tie, navy trousers, brown shoes, modest rounded belly with realistic shirt folds over it, arms relaxed at sides, delighted open mouth. TOP MIDDLE: substantially plump employee after second promotion in navy suit, teal tie, round belly with open jacket curving around it, heavier cheeks, natural shoulder/arm/leg proportions, hands at sides, open smiling mouth. TOP RIGHT: very heavy employee after third promotion, cream suit, gold tie, rounded belly projecting forward, curved shirt hem, well tailored folds, thick thighs and sturdy stance, normal length arms at sides, open smiling mouth. BOTTOM ROW: all three sprites depict the SAME even heavier, round-bellied young employee as the new CEO in cream suit with gold tie, recognisable hair/face with full cheeks, large belly like a corpulent boss. BOTTOM LEFT both fists raised in celebration, feet on ground. BOTTOM MIDDLE holding lapels proudly, feet on ground. BOTTOM RIGHT sitting facing camera in burgundy leather office chair, both feet apart on ground, hands on armrests, no crossed legs. Full chair visible, no cropping. All sprites natural round volume, not flat stretched bodies. Clean alpha edges, no white halo. Preserve coherent game character identity, clothing colors, crisp sprite readability.

## Elevator prompt

Reference image is the existing office game background: match its moody detailed pixel-art, warm lamps, dark walnut paneling, bronze metal trim, subtle texture, not a flat vector illustration. Create exactly TWO equally sized side-by-side panels on a transparent canvas, 1536x1024. The SAME front-facing office elevator, same camera, scale, frame alignment and geometry in both panels. LEFT panel elevator CLOSED, two brushed dark bronze sliding door leaves with a narrow central vertical seam. RIGHT panel elevator OPEN, both doors fully retracted into the frame, empty welcoming cabin visible, walnut back wall, brass waist-height handrail and warm cream ceiling light, dark carpet and shallow perspective floor. Each complete elevator portal frame fits within its 768x1024 cell with clear transparent margins all around. Flat front-facing architectural elevation; symmetrical, heavy outer frame, thin brass edge highlights, restrained art-deco geometric accents. Door opening a simple straight rectangle, approx from x=160 to608 and y=180 to910 in each local cell. Portal outer frame approx x=95 to675 and y=90 to965. Small blank dark rectangular floor indicator centered above doorway, blank call-button plate on right outer jamb. NO numbers, NO words, NO people, NO labels, NO external building or scenery, NO text, NO neon. Seamless clean alpha silhouette. Both panels pixel-aligned, suitable for slicing door leaves and sliding them apart in code.
